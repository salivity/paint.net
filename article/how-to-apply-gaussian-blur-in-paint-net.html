How to Apply Gaussian Blur in Paint.NET

Applying a Gaussian Blur in Paint.NET is a quick and effective way to soften an image, conceal sensitive information, or create an artificial depth-of-field effect. This guide walks you through the straightforward process of finding and using the built-in Gaussian Blur tool, whether you want to blur an entire photo or just a selected portion.

Step 1: Open Your Image

Launch Paint.NET and open the image you want to edit by clicking File > Open, or by dragging and dropping the file directly into the workspace.

Step 2: Select an Area (Optional)

If you want to blur the entire image or layer, skip to the next step. If you only want to blur a specific object, face, or section of text:

  1. Choose a selection tool from the Tools palette, such as the Rectangle Select, Ellipse Select, or Lasso Select.
  2. Click and drag over the specific area you want to blur. Only the contents inside the active selection boundary will be affected.

Step 3: Open the Gaussian Blur Tool

With your image (or selection) ready:

  1. Click on the Effects menu at the top of the screen.
  2. Hover over the Blurs sub-menu.
  3. Select Gaussian Blur... from the list.

Step 4: Adjust the Blur Radius

A dialog window will appear displaying a Radius slider:

  1. Drag the slider to the right to increase the blur intensity, or to the left to reduce it. You can also type a specific number into the input box.
  2. Watch the canvas in real time to preview the effect. A smaller radius (1–5) adds a subtle softening, while a higher radius completely obscures details.

Step 5: Apply and Save

  1. Once you are satisfied with the look, click OK to apply the effect.
  2. If you made a selection, press Ctrl + D to deselect the area.
  3. Save your finished work by going to File > Save or File > Save As.
