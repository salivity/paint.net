Shortcut to Zoom In on Paint.NET

Navigating your canvas efficiently in Paint.NET is essential for detailed editing and graphic design. This guide covers the quickest keyboard and mouse shortcuts used to zoom in on the canvas, along with useful related controls to help streamline your workflow.

The Primary Zoom In Shortcut

The fastest keyboard shortcut to zoom in on the canvas in Paint.NET is:

  • Ctrl + Plus (+)

Pressing the Ctrl key and the + (plus) key simultaneously increases the magnification level incrementally. You can use either the plus key on your main keyboard or the + key on the numeric keypad.

Zooming with the Mouse

If you prefer using a mouse, you can quickly zoom in and out without reaching across the keyboard:

  • Ctrl + Mouse Wheel Up: Hold down the Ctrl key and scroll the mouse wheel forward (up) to zoom in directly centered on your mouse cursor's position.

Using the Zoom Tool

You can also activate the dedicated Zoom Tool via a single-key shortcut:

  1. Press Z on your keyboard to switch to the Zoom tool.
  2. Left-click anywhere on the canvas to zoom in on that specific point.
  3. Alternatively, click and drag a rectangular selection box with the Zoom tool to instantly magnify a specific area.

Helpful Related Shortcuts

To fully control your canvas view, keep these complementary shortcuts in mind:

  • Zoom Out: Press Ctrl + Minus (-) or hold Ctrl and scroll the Mouse Wheel Down.
  • Fit to Window: Press Ctrl + B to automatically resize the canvas view to fit your current screen dimensions.
  • Actual Size (100%): Press Ctrl + Shift + A to return the zoom level to true 100% scale.
