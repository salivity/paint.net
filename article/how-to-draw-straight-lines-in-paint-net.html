How to Draw Straight Lines in Paint.NET

This article explains how to draw perfectly straight horizontal, vertical, and angled lines in Paint.NET using built-in modifier keys. You will learn how the Shift key restricts the Line/Curve tool to precise angle increments, ensuring smooth and accurate lines without manual alignment.

The Primary Modifier Key: Shift

In Paint.NET, the primary modifier key used for drawing straight lines is the Shift key.

When you select the Line/Curve tool (shortcut: O) and drag on the canvas:

  • Holding down the Shift key automatically snaps and constrains the angle of your line to increments of 15 degrees (such as 0°, 15°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 75°, and 90°).
  • This makes it easy to draw perfectly level horizontal lines, plumb vertical lines, or exact 45-degree diagonals.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select the Line/Curve tool from the Tools palette or press O on your keyboard.
  2. Choose your desired brush width and style from the toolbar at the top.
  3. Click and hold the left mouse button where you want the line to start.
  4. Press and hold the Shift key on your keyboard.
  5. Drag your mouse to the desired endpoint. The line will snap to the nearest 15-degree increment.
  6. Release the mouse button first, then release the Shift key.

Adjusting Line Endpoints

After drawing a line, Paint.NET places control nubs at both ends:

  • You can click and drag either endpoint nub to resize or reposition the line.
  • If you hold Shift while dragging a nub, the 15-degree angle constraint remains active, keeping your straight line aligned while adjusting its length.

Mouse Button Modifiers

  • Left Mouse Button: Draws the line using your active Primary Color.
  • Right Mouse Button: Draws the line using your active Secondary Color.
