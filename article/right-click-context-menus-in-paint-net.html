Right-Click Context Menus in Paint.NET

In Paint.NET, right-clicking directly on the canvas does not open a traditional pop-up context menu. Instead, the software reserves the right mouse button for secondary tool actions, such as drawing with your secondary color or modifying selection shapes. This guide explains how right-clicking functions on the canvas, why the standard context menu is absent, and how to quickly access the contextual tools and editing commands you need.

Understanding Right-Click Behavior on the Canvas

Paint.NET assigns specific functional commands to the right mouse button depending on the tool you currently have active:

  • Drawing Tools (Paintbrush, Pencil, Shapes): Right-clicking and dragging applies your active Secondary Color instead of your Primary Color.
  • Selection Tools (Rectangle, Lasso, Ellipse): Dragging with the right mouse button subtracts an area from your current selection rather than creating a new one.
  • Move Selection and Move Selected Pixels: Holding the right mouse button allows you to rotate the selection or the active pixels around a central anchor point.
  • Gradient Tool: Right-clicking creates a gradient blend that starts with your Secondary Color and transitions to transparent or the Primary Color, depending on your blend mode.
  • Color Picker: Right-clicking on any pixel samples that color directly into your Secondary Color slot.

How to Access Contextual Options in Paint.NET

Because canvas right-clicks are dedicated to drawing and manipulation mechanics, you must access contextual commands through alternative interface locations:

1. The Tool Options Bar

Whenever you select a tool from the Tools palette, the contextual settings for that tool appear horizontally in the Tool Options bar just beneath the main application menu. Here you can adjust brush sizes, hardness, fill styles, blending modes, and selection behaviors.

2. Utility Panel Context Menus

While the canvas itself lacks a right-click menu, several of Paint.NET’s floating utility panels support them:

  • Layers Panel: Right-click any layer in the panel to quickly open the Layer Properties dialog, where you can adjust layer blending, visibility, and opacity.
  • Colors Panel: Right-click color swatches to assign them directly to your Secondary Color slot.

3. Keyboard Shortcuts for Rapid Editing

To perform common context-menu tasks without navigating top-level menus, use standard keyboard shortcuts:

  • Deselect: Ctrl + D
  • Invert Selection: Ctrl + I
  • Crop to Selection: Ctrl + Shift + X
  • Cut, Copy, Paste: Ctrl + X, Ctrl + C, Ctrl + V
  • Paste into New Layer: Ctrl + Shift + V
