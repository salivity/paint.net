How to Scroll Horizontally in Paint.NET

Navigating a large image in Paint.NET often requires moving from side to side quickly without having to reach for the scrollbars. This guide covers the exact shortcut to scroll horizontally across the canvas using your mouse wheel, along with a few alternative methods to help streamline your navigation workflow.

Use the Shift Key and Mouse Wheel

The primary and fastest way to scroll horizontally in Paint.NET using your mouse wheel is by combining it with the Shift key:

  1. Move your mouse cursor anywhere over the active canvas.
  2. Press and hold the Shift key on your keyboard.
  3. Roll the mouse wheel down to scroll to the right.
  4. Roll the mouse wheel up to scroll to the left.

Release the Shift key when you want the mouse wheel to return to its standard vertical scrolling behavior.

Alternative Ways to Pan Horizontally

If you want alternative methods for moving horizontally across your artwork, Paint.NET includes several other convenient options:

  • Click and Drag with the Middle Mouse Button: Press and hold down the middle mouse button (the scroll wheel itself) and drag the cursor left or right to pan across the canvas in any direction.
  • Spacebar Pan: Hold down the Spacebar to temporarily switch to the Pan tool, then left-click and drag the canvas left or right.
  • Hardware Tilt Wheel: If your mouse has a scroll wheel with horizontal tilt functionality, pushing the wheel to the left or right will automatically scroll the canvas in that direction without holding any keys.
  • Canvas Scrollbar: You can manually click and drag the horizontal scrollbar located along the bottom edge of the image viewport.
